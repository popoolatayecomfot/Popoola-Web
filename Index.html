How I Built This

I started with a mobile-first approach because most people browse on their phones. For the layout, I used Flexbox to stack elements on small screens and switch to a side-by-side view on desktop.

The hardest part was getting the mobile menu to work. I used JavaScript’s classList.toggle to show and hide the menu when the hamburger button is clicked.
